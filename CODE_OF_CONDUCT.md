# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Communicate respectfully, discuss technical decisions in good faith, accept
evidence-based correction, and keep feedback focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit
participation, or ban participants when behavior threatens a safe and productive
project environment. Report concerns privately to the maintainer rather than in
a public issue.
